A smaller AI bill can still mean a higher cost per accepted result.

In this fictional teaching example:
Manual: 24 units / 3 accepted results = 8.00 each.
AI: 18.40 units / 2 accepted results = 9.20 each.

The two ledgers have the same three recorded result IDs. Those IDs do not establish a fair experiment or the quality of an answer. Define the actual task, population, period, currency, acceptance rule and cost allocation consistently before comparing.

Count failed attempts, retries and review costs too. Unknown costs are input errors; no accepted results gives an undefined unit cost, rather than zero. Keep accepted-result counts beside the bill.
